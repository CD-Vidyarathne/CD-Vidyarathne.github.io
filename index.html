About

The short version

I’m a backend-leaning full-stack engineer with about three years of professional and freelance experience. I’ve built and run enterprise services, owned features from design through production, reviewed code and mentored junior engineers, and worked closely with architects on technical decisions.

I’m strongest in Kotlin and Java, especially reactive WebFlux and virtual-thread MVC services, and in TypeScript with Next.js. I like AI features that are practical: retrieval, tool calling and well-designed prompts inside real products, not demos.
